About Destination

Known as the 'Queen of Hill Stations', Ooty features picturesque tea gardens, serene lakes, cool climate, and the heritage Nilgiri Mountain Railway. A beloved colonial getaway set in the Nilgiri Hills.

Transit Guide & How to Reach

By Train

Nearest major broad-gauge station: Mettupalayam (MTP). Connect via Nilgiri Express or take the UNESCO Toy Train directly to Ooty.

Book on IRCTC

By Bus

Frequent direct overnight KSRTC & SETC AC sleeper buses from Bangalore (8 hrs), Chennai (10 hrs), and Coimbatore (3 hrs).

Search on RedBus

By Air

Nearest airport: Coimbatore International Airport (CJB), located 88 km away. Pre-paid cabs take approx 3.5 hours up the ghats.
